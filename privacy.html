IRC Incentive privacy policy

IRC Incentive is a private desktop tool. It has no servers of its own.

Google data

After you sign in, the app uses your Google account to see your email address, check your rights on the incentive sheet, read that sheet, and, only if your account can edit it and you approve a batch or delivery, add rows and change cell formatting there. It does not use other files in your Drive.

Where data is kept

Your sign-in and a local copy of what the app reads stay on your own computer. The app does not send Google data to its developer or to anyone else, and does not sell it or use it for advertising.

Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Use Sign out in the app's Settings, or remove the app from your Google account's third-party access page.
